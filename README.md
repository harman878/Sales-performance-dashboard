📊 Sales Performance Dashboard | Microsoft Excel
Overview

This project demonstrates how Excel can be used to transform raw sales data into an interactive business dashboard. Using the Microsoft Financial Sample dataset, I analyzed sales performance, profitability, product performance, regional trends, and the impact of discounts to create a dashboard that supports data-driven decision-making.

The dashboard combines data cleaning, automation, and interactive reporting to help users quickly explore key business metrics and identify opportunities for improvement.

Business Objectives

The dashboard was built to answer important business questions, including:

Which countries and customer segments contribute the most to revenue?
Which products generate the highest sales and profit?
How do different discount bands affect profitability?
How has sales performance changed over time?
How can business performance be monitored through an interactive dashboard?

Key Business Insights
High discounts did not always translate into higher profitability, highlighting the importance of balancing pricing strategies with profit margins.
A small number of products contributed a significant share of overall sales, making them key drivers of business performance.
Sales performance varied across countries and market segments, indicating that business growth opportunities differ by region and customer type.
Monthly sales trends revealed recurring periods of stronger performance, suggesting seasonal demand patterns that can support inventory and sales planning.
Some customer segments generated strong revenue but comparatively lower profit, showing that sales volume alone is not always the best measure of business success.
Interactive filtering made it easy to identify top-performing combinations of products, regions, and customer segments, enabling faster business reporting and decision-making.
