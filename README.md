# 📊 Sales Performance Dashboard | Microsoft Excel

## 📌 Overview

This project demonstrates how Microsoft Excel can be used to transform raw sales data into an interactive business dashboard. Using the Microsoft Financial Sample dataset, I analyzed sales performance, profitability, product performance, regional trends, and the impact of discounts to create a dashboard that supports data-driven decision-making.

The dashboard combines data cleaning, automation, and interactive reporting to help users quickly explore key business metrics and identify opportunities for improvement.

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives

The dashboard was built to answer key business questions such as:

- Which countries and customer segments contribute the most to revenue?
- Which products generate the highest sales and profit?
- How do different discount bands affect profitability?
- How has sales performance changed over time?
- How can business performance be monitored through an interactive dashboard?

## 📈 Dashboard Features

- 📊 Interactive KPI cards for tracking key business metrics.
- 🎛️ Dynamic slicers for filtering data by Product, Country, Segment, and Year.
- 📅 Sales and profit trend analysis across different time periods.
- 🌍 Regional and product performance comparison.
- 💰 Discount band analysis to evaluate pricing impact.
- ⚡ One-click dashboard refresh using VBA automation.
- 🎨 Clean, interactive, and user-friendly dashboard design.

---

## 💡 Key Business Insights

- High discount levels did not always result in higher profitability, highlighting the importance of balancing pricing strategies with profit margins.
- A small number of products contributed a significant share of total sales, making them the primary drivers of business performance.
- Sales performance varied across countries and customer segments, indicating that growth opportunities differ by region and market.
- Monthly sales trends revealed recurring periods of stronger performance, suggesting seasonal demand patterns that can support inventory and sales planning.
- Some customer segments generated high revenue but comparatively lower profit, showing that sales volume alone is not always the best indicator of business success.
